FROM AN IDEA TO EVIDENCE.

Build a thoughtful detector.

The inspection workflow is ready. A trained model is the next step.

Start with what the app can verify.

Without a trained checkpoint, TruthLens reads media, samples frames, measures image quality, and creates a report. It does not assign an authenticity label or invent a confidence score.

01

Prepare labeled data

Collect examples of authentic, AI-generated, and manipulated images from sources you are permitted to use.

  • Keep separate training, validation, and test sets.
  • Split by source to reduce data leakage.
  • Document image origins and labeling decisions.
02

Train & evaluate

Use the repository's training workflow to build a baseline and test it on data it has never seen.

  • Compare performance across all three classes.
  • Check compression, resizing, and new sources.
  • Load the evaluated checkpoint into the app.
03

Inspect with care

Review predictions alongside the original media, sampled frames, and model attention.

  • A score depends on the model and its data.
  • Video uses sampled frames, not every frame.
  • Verify important claims with other evidence.

Three classes. One careful interpretation.

Authentic · AI-generated · Manipulated

Real media may be misclassified, and new generation methods may go undetected. Technical quality measurements are separate from detection predictions.
